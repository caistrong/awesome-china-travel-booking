# Awesome China Travel Booking

> A curated, practical list of **how foreign visitors actually book China's hardest attractions**: release times, passport real-name rules, what works without a Chinese phone number, and how to pay.

China's big sights (the Forbidden City, the National Museum, Tiananmen Square, the Terracotta Army, the Panda Base) don't sell out because they are expensive. They sell out because tickets are **released at a fixed minute, tied to one passport, and often sold through apps that assume a Chinese phone number**. This list collects the booking path that works for each place for someone with a foreign passport, plus the traps that catch people.

Maintained by [Xiaocong](https://simplechinatrip.com/about/), a local in Shenzhen who runs [Simple China Trip](https://simplechinatrip.com), a free, independent booking guide in 8 languages. Most facts below were checked on official sites between 5 and 8 October 2026. **Rules change, so always confirm on the official booking page before you pay.**

Not affiliated with any attraction. No ticket sales and no affiliate links in this repo.

## The five rules that matter everywhere

1. **Beijing time is the only clock.** "Tickets open at 20:00" means 20:00 in UTC+8, not on a phone that is still set to your home time zone.
2. **Real-name, one passport per ticket.** Most major sights tie each ticket to a passport name and number. Bring the *same physical passport* to the gate. A photo or screenshot is usually not enough.
3. **One ticket per passport per day** is common (Forbidden City, Panda Base, Universal Beijing). You cannot hold "spare" tickets on your own passport.
4. **Prefer the official English site, then the overseas channel the attraction itself links to.** Avoid the WeChat QR on a homepage when there is a separate foreigner button.
5. **No-shows can get you banned.** Some sights count late cancellations and no-shows as strikes (details below). Cancel properly if your plans change.

## Beijing

### Forbidden City (Palace Museum)

- **Where:** the official site `bookingticket.dpm.org.cn` (switch to EN). Sign in with **email + verification code**; no Chinese phone or WeChat needed.
- **When:** **20:00 Beijing time, 7 days before** your visit. No same-day tickets on the official site. Closed Mondays (except national holidays).
- **Rules:** passport name and number for each visitor; one ticket per passport per day; up to 10 per order. Morning or afternoon session (morning sells out first).
- **Gate:** enter only through the Meridian Gate (Wumen, south side) with the same passport.
- **Watch out:** 3 no-shows in 180 days blocks you from booking for 60 days. Refunded tickets do not go back on sale, so don't wait for "cancellations".
- Guide: [How to book Forbidden City tickets as a foreigner](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/forbidden-city-tickets-foreigners/)
- Sold out? [Forbidden City tickets sold out: what to do](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/forbidden-city-tickets-sold-out/) (batch release after 20:00, the afternoon session, the museum's email route, guided tours)

### National Museum of China

- Free, but needs a reservation. English portal `pcticket.chnmuseum.cn/museum-en`, then email + code login.
- Opens **17:00, up to 7 days ahead**. Three time slots per day.
- Guide: [National Museum of China reservation with a passport](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/national-museum-china-reservation-passport/)

### Tiananmen Square

- Free, but needs its own real-name reservation **1–7 days ahead**; new dates appear at **12:00**. Walk-ups without a booking are routinely refused.
- The official channel is a WeChat mini-program that often asks for Chinese SMS. The common fix: ask your **hotel front desk** to book with your passport details.
- A Forbidden City ticket is not a reliable substitute for square entry.
- Guides: [Tiananmen Square reservation](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/tiananmen-square-reservation-foreigners/) · [Mao Memorial Hall](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/mao-mausoleum-tickets-foreigners/)

### Great Wall: Mutianyu and Badaling

- **Mutianyu** has its own English site (`en.mutianyugreatwall.com`), books up to 15 days ahead and lets you reach the passport form without logging in. Most people want at least the shuttle: the wall is about 3 km from the parking and service area.
- **Badaling**: on `badaling.cn` use the **外籍人购票** (foreigner tickets) button, which opens the official Trip.com listing. The homepage 预约购票 QR is for domestic WeChat users.
- Guides: [Mutianyu tickets](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/mutianyu-great-wall-tickets-foreigners/) · [Badaling vs Mutianyu](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/badaling-vs-mutianyu-tickets/)

### Walk-up sights

Summer Palace, Temple of Heaven, Jingshan Park and Beihai Park usually don't need advance booking: buy at the gate with your passport, which also makes them good backups when the Forbidden City is full.

- [Beijing parks without reservation](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/beijing-parks-foreigners-no-reservation/) · [Summer Palace](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/summer-palace-tickets-foreigners/) · [Temple of Heaven](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/temple-of-heaven-tickets-foreigners/) · [Lama Temple](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/lama-temple-tickets-foreigners/)

## Shanghai

## Xi'an

- **Terracotta Army**: book with ID type **Passport**. On Trip.com choose the non-mainland-residents ticket that includes Lishan Garden. Official face value CNY 120 adult / CNY 60 student. Enter via the North Gate with the original passport. The Trip.com flow allows one ticket per ID within 8 days.
  Guide: [Terracotta Army tickets for foreigners](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/terracotta-army-tickets-foreigners/)
- **Shaanxi History Museum**: free main museum, **WeChat only**, passport accepted. Release **17:00, 5 days ahead**. Max 4 bookings per ID in 30 days, and a no-show without cancelling means a 180-day ban. The Trip.com "museum ticket" is a paid special hall, not general admission.
  Guide: [Shaanxi History Museum tickets](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/shaanxi-history-museum-tickets-foreigners/)
- More: [Xi'an City Wall](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/xian-city-wall-tickets-foreigners/)

## Tibet

- **Potala Palace**: foreigners need a **Tibet Travel Permit**, which only a licensed Tibet travel agency can apply for. The agency books your timed palace ticket. Agencies commonly cite 8–9 business days for the permit, so book your tour about 20 days ahead. There are no same-day tickets.

## No Chinese phone number?

Many official systems were built for Chinese ID cards and +86 SMS, but several big ones work fine without:

| Works with email or a passport form | Usually needs +86 SMS (plan B: hotel desk) |
|---|---|
| Forbidden City (email code) | Tiananmen Square (WeChat) |
| National Museum of China (email code) | Sanxingdui Museum (WeChat) |
| Mutianyu Great Wall (passport form) | Some city museum and park mini-programs |
| Shanghai Disneyland (official EN site) | |

- If you plan to book many WeChat-only sights yourself, get a China SIM or eSIM that **explicitly supports receiving SMS**. Data-only eSIMs often fail verification.
- Guides: [Booking without a Chinese phone number](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/china-attraction-booking-without-chinese-phone/) · [China SMS for attraction booking](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/china-sms-for-attraction-booking/) · [WeChat mini-program vs English web](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/wechat-miniprogram-vs-english-web-foreigners/)

## Payments: Alipay and WeChat Pay for tourists

Do this **before you fly**, while your home SIM still receives SMS:

1. Install Alipay and WeChat and register with your international number.
2. Complete **passport identity verification** (photo page + live face check).
3. Link a **Visa or Mastercard** (tell your bank to allow overseas and online spending).
4. Try a tiny payment at home so declines show up before you land.

Older blogs still recommend Alipay "Tour Pass". That prepaid wallet is no longer the path for new users: link your foreign card directly in the main app instead. Check any service fee in the app on the day you pay.

- Guide: [Alipay and WeChat Pay for tourists](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/alipay-wechat-pay-tourists/)

## Visa-free trip checklist

- Confirm your eligibility on **official** MFA or embassy sources for your nationality. Visa-free and 240-hour transit rules change, including end dates.
- Being visa-free does **not** skip attraction reservations.
- Book hotels that clearly accept foreign passports. Hotels must register foreign guests with the police on your first night.
- Set calendar alarms in Beijing time for the clocks that matter: 20:00 (Forbidden City), 17:00 (National Museum, Shaanxi History Museum), 12:00 (Tiananmen dates), 08:00 (Lingyin).
- Guide: [Visa-free China booking checklist](https://simplechinatrip.com/guides/china-visa-free-booking-checklist/)

## Scams and pitfalls

- Never send passport photos to unofficial "helpers" on chat apps.
- Don't buy tickets from touts or chat-app sellers. Tickets are real-name, so a ticket in someone else's name won't get you in.
- The Palace Museum says it has authorised **no** third-party sellers of plain admission tickets. Guided tours that include entry are a different product (they use a separate agency quota).
- Watch for one-digit passport typos. Fix them with the seller (or at Guest Services for theme parks) before queueing.
- Some parks list many Trip.com products. Prefer the one the attraction itself links to, or the one labelled for non-mainland residents.
